WORD CLOUD

drag · combine · level up

Drag a syllable bubble. The cloud's gravity pulls everything toward the centre; the others jostle out of the way as you move. When the bubble you're dragging touches another and the two spell a dictionary word (forward or reversed), a green halo lights both pieces.

Release to lock the move in — if a word formed, the pair burns and scores. Hit Space to send the next syllable falling in from outside the cloud. If the cloud grows so dense that a new arrival can't settle within the play radius, the run is over.

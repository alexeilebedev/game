10,000 MONKEYS

a syllable shooter

You're shooting syllables — chunks of 2, 3, or 4 letters drawn from the language by frequency. When the letters in a row, column, or diagonal spell a dictionary word (≥ 4 letters), the parent syllable bubbles burn, and unsupported bubbles fall for cascade combos.

Each shot adds 1 to the penalty meter. Burning a word relieves the penalty by (letters − 2) — a 4-letter word is worth 2, a 6-letter word 4, an 8-letter word 6. When penalty reaches 10, the grid shifts down; if it reaches the cannon, the game ends.
